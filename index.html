About

I spend my days writing code that has to be exact. A robot-worlds simulation doesn't forgive an off-by-one error, and neither does a runway turn. That's the throughline: precision under pressure, whether it's a terminal or a lighting rig.

I'm finishing an Occupational Certificate in Software Engineering at WeThinkCode_, where I also tutor other students, so I'm used to being watched, corrected, and performing under scrutiny, which is most of what modeling actually is. High fashion appeals to me for the same reason systems programming does: it rewards discipline, and it punishes anyone faking it.
